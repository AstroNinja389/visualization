A twistless converse theorem

Pictures of how the functional equations of twists by Ramanujan sums pin down a modular form, and where they fall short.

Hecke's converse theorem

Why twists?

The semicircles in which a functional equation lifts a point cover the line only for N ≤ 4. Ramanujan-sum twists fill the gaps, except at the cusps of the excluded levels.

The spanning theorem

The matrix of legal rows, level by level. Where the theorem allows, the rank climbs to Q; at the excluded levels an explicit vector is missed by every row.

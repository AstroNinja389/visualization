The spanning theorem

What the legal rows look like, level by level
  • Columns: the circle \(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z\) cut into \(Q\) cells \([\tfrac jQ,\tfrac{j+1}Q]\), drawn from \(-\tfrac12\) to \(\tfrac12\).
  • Rows: for \(q\mid Q\), the row \(R^Q_{q,u}\) has a \(1\) in each cell \(u+aQ/q\) with \(\gcd(a,q)=1\). It is legal when \(\max\{|u|,|u+1|\}<Q/\sqrt{\operatorname{lcm}(N,q^2)}\): the cell \(u\) lies under the semicircle of the \(c_q\)-twist.
  • Theorem: for some \(Q\) the legal rows span \(\mathbb Q^{\mathbb Z/Q\mathbb Z}\) if and only if \(16\nmid N\) and \(p^2\nmid N\) for every odd prime \(p\).

Reading the picture

  • Each row is one twisted functional equation used on one cell: it controls the sum of \(f\) over the marked cells.
  • Rows with \(q=1,2\) have a single \(1\) (teal); rows with \(q\ge3\) have one \(1\) for each fraction \(a/q\) (purple).
  • Each \(q\)-block is a band of parallel diagonals, one for each \(a\).
  • The bar under the matrix is the rank of the rows so far; the rows span when it reaches \(Q\).
  • The strip at the top shows the circle covered by the semicircles with \(q\mid Q\): teal for \(q\le2\), purple where only \(q\ge3\) reach.

Levels that span

  • The admissible \(N\le70\) from the paper's table whose smallest \(Q\) is at most \(1596\), each drawn at that smallest \(Q\).
  • Spanning means every value of \(f\) near the line is a combination of values higher up.

Excluded levels

  • If \(s\) is an odd prime with \(s^2\mid N\), or \(s=4\) with \(16\mid N\), take \(Q\) divisible by \(s\).
  • The vector with \(\sin(2\pi m/s)\) in the cell \(mQ/s\) (amber columns) and \(0\) elsewhere is orthogonal to every legal row.
  • So the rows never span, for any \(Q\). This is the imaginary part of the vector in the paper's proof of necessity.

Ranks computed modulo the prime \(2^{61}-1\) (full rank modulo a prime implies full rank over \(\mathbb Q\)). For every level that spans, the rank is \(Q\) at the table's \(Q\) and below \(Q'\) at \(Q'=Q/p\) for each prime \(p\mid Q\), as the table says. For the excluded levels the orthogonality of the amber vector to all legal rows was checked numerically (largest pairing below \(10^{-15}\)).
